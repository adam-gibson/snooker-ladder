    Ladder rules
    • You can challenge the player 1 or 2 places above you, as long as neither of you is already in a challenge.
    • The challenged player must accept. The match (best of 3 frames) must be played within 240 hours (10 days) of the challenge. The site will not accept a booking after the deadline.
    • Challenger beats the player directly above: they swap places.
    • Challenger beats the player 2 places above: the challenger takes that place, the beaten player drops 1, and the player in between drops into the challenger's old place.
    • Challenger loses: nobody moves.
    • If no result is entered by the deadline, it counts as a win for the challenger and the ladder updates automatically.
    • Either player enters the score with their PIN. The other player confirms it, or it is confirmed automatically after 24 hours.
    • To leave the ladder, ask the admin in the WhatsApp group. Everyone below moves up one place.

    Phone alerts (free)

    Install the ntfy app (Android or iPhone), tap + and subscribe to the topic the admin sends you. You will get an alert whenever you are challenged or a result needs confirming.
